# 📜 Project Charter – STM Football Merchandise Shop

## 1. Purpose
The STM Football Merchandise Shop project aims to create a management system that simplifies product catalog management, customer shopping, billing, and inventory tracking. The system will also provide analytics to support business growth and decision-making.

---

## 2. Objectives
- Provide secure login and registration for customers and administrators.  
- Display a product catalog with search and filter options.  
- Enable shopping cart and checkout functionality.  
- Automate billing and receipt generation.  
- Track inventory levels and generate restock alerts.  
- Provide sales and performance reports for shop management.  

---

## 3. Scope
**In Scope:**  
- Customer login and registration.  
- Product catalog and shopping cart.  
- Billing and receipt generation.  
- Inventory management with stock alerts.  
- Reporting dashboard.  

**Out of Scope:**  
- Integration with external marketplaces (future consideration).  
- Mobile app version (future enhancement).
