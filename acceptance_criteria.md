# ✅ Acceptance Criteria – STM Football Merchandise Shop

## 1. Login
- Customers must log in with email + password.  
- Passwords stored securely with hashing.  
- Account lock after 5 failed attempts.  
- Successful login redirects to dashboard within 2 seconds.  

---

## 2. Performance
- System supports 100 concurrent users.  
- Product search responds in <2 seconds.  
- Checkout completes in <5 seconds.  
- Reports generate within 5 seconds for datasets up to 10,000 records.  

---

## 3. Data Storage
- Customer and order records stored securely.  
- Billing records encrypted at rest.  
- Daily backups must occur automatically.  
- Deleted records must not be recoverable by unauthorized users.  

---

## 4. Inventory
- Low-stock alerts trigger when stock <10 items.  
- Inventory updates automatically after each sale.  
- Restock date must be recorded for each item.  

---

## 5. Billing
- Receipts generated instantly after checkout.  
- Discounts applied correctly to total amount.  
- Payment records stored securely.
